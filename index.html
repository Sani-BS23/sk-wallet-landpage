SK Wallet

See total money, card bills, loans, and investments in one place. Add daily entries from a floating plus — banks, cash, bKash, and Nagad included.

What it covers

Built for day-to-day money in Bangladesh — Taka amounts, bank and mobile wallet accounts, credit cards, EMIs, and money you put into investments.

Report

Total bank and cash, card and loan debt, and income / expense / investment for the month you pick.

Banks & cards

Cash, banks, bKash, Nagad, and credit cards with bill day, pay day, and outstanding.

Entries, loans, invest

Quick add from the plus button. Track EMIs and money lent. Log investment areas without live prices.

Screen-by-screen guide

Same screens as the live app. Use this as a short user manual after you install.

01 · Report

Your money at a glance

Report opens first. The blue card is total money (bank + cash). Below that: owed on cards, owed on loans, and money owed to you.

  • Switch Month, Year, or All — or open the calendar for a custom range.
  • Use the arrows to move through October (or any month).
  • Income, Expense, and Investment totals follow the period you selected.
  • Header icons: dark mode, categories, and your profile.
02 · Bank and cards

Accounts that hold the balance

Add cash or a bank with a name and opening balance. bKash and Nagad count as banks. A debit card is the same account as its bank — credit cards sit under Cards.

  • Tap + Add account for cash or bank.
  • Fund Transfer moves money between accounts.
  • Add Credit Card for bill day, pay day, limit, and outstanding.
  • Edit or delete from the pencil and trash on each card row.
03 · Transactions

History you can filter

Every income, expense, investment, and card pay shows here. Filter by Last 30, Day, Month, Year, or a custom calendar range, then narrow by category.

  • All categories is the default — open the dropdown to focus one category.
  • Tap a row’s pencil to edit, trash to remove.
  • The floating + opens Add entry from any main tab.
04 · Add entry

Log money in seconds

Tap the blue + button. Choose Expense, Income, Investment, or Card pay, enter the amount, pick a category, choose where it came from (or went), set the date, and Save.

  • Categories: Food, Transport, Rent, Bills, Shopping, Other (you can manage more from Categories).
  • Spent from needs at least one cash or bank account.
  • Note is optional. Close with the X if you change your mind.
05 · Loans

EMI, loans, and money lent

Track what you still owe and what others owe you. Paid loans lock so you do not edit them by mistake — reopen when you need to.

  • Tap + Add loan to start an EMI, normal loan, or money lent.
  • The list icon switches between running loans and all (including paid).
  • Paid rows appear muted so active debt stays easy to see.
06 · Investment

Money you put in

Create areas like Stock market, DPS, or SIP and record what you contributed. The app does not look up live market prices — it tracks what you put in.

  • Tap + Add area to name a bucket.
  • Add contributions from Add entry → Investment, or from inside an area.
  • Totals roll into the Report investment line for your selected period.

Install on Android

Use the latest preview build. Install over the SK Wallet that already has your records — do not uninstall first.

Open install page Direct APK
  1. Open the install page on your Android phone (or download the APK).
  2. Allow the browser to install apps if Android asks.
  3. Install over the existing SK Wallet (com.myfirstapp) so balances and history stay.
  4. If an empty second SK Wallet is still on the phone from an older package id, you can remove that empty one after the main app is updated.

iPhone install needs a registered device and a separate iOS preview build. Ask when you are ready for that.
